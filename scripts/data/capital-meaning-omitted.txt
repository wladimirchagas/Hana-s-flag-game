IT-VS  # Medio Campidano/Sud Sardegna (Villacidro): the bundled IT-VS flag shows a red-castle arms under a mural crown, which is NOT Villacidro's coat of arms — Villacidro's arms (granted 1963, per it.wikipedia/Villacidro) are the Sa Spendula waterfall, a roebuck on pine, and a fruited citrus tree, with no castle (the castle arms are the co-seat Sanluri's). Flag/city mismatch: a Villacidro flag-meaning cannot be sourced for this castle image, so it is omitted rather than mislabelled.

CV-PR  # Praia: the bundled flag is the modern Cidade da Praia arms (castle, lighthouse, tree, sea, stars). The only documented Praia heraldry (heraldicacivica.pt, Portuguese-language) is the older COLONIAL arms — a different design (Quinas, ten stars, mitre/crosier) — with no per-element symbolism, and the modern arms could not be confirmed to match any documented blazon. pt-language searches + heraldicacivica checked.
